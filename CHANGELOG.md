# Changelog

## 2026-06-07

- **`Home`: tariff-period line** — a second line under `Écran` shows the current pricing period and the exact time the meter switched to it, e.g. `HC depuis 13:00` (off-peak since 13:00). Unlike the removed EDF title dot (stale by design between refreshes), this reads as a fact: the **wall-clock moment of the last HC↔HP transition** observed live on the ZLinky's `PTEC` field. The first frame after a server start only baselines the period (not a switch), so the line stays **absent until a real transition is seen** — no `HC_WINDOWS` clock guess, no DB persistence; a restart blanks it until the next flip (at most a few hours). Goldens and preview regenerated; no new environment variable.

## 2026-06-06

- **`EDF`: tariff-period dot removed** — the live HC/HP dot after the `EDF` title is gone. The pricing period flips on a sub-hour schedule while the screen only refreshes every few hours, so the dot was stale most of the time and conveyed nothing reliable. Removes the dot rendering plus the `off_peak_now` plumbing (the off-peak detection itself stays — the power sensors' HC split relies on it). Goldens and preview regenerated; no environment variable change.
- **`Eau` & `Solaire` charts: intraday strips** — the Water and Solar charts gain the same mini bar-graph under each daily bar as the EDF chart (6 four-hour sparkline-style bars between the bar and its day label, space always reserved). Neither domain stored sub-daily data, so each gains its own samples table filled live: **`solar_samples`** keeps the inverter heartbeats' mean PV watts per 30-min slot (flushed at slot boundaries, like the TIC), **`water_samples`** keeps the meter's cumulative index per slot, diffed into litres at read time (midnight baseline from the previous day, quiet-slot jumps land on the next reporting slot, meter resets re-baseline silently). Buckets average the power profiles but **sum** the litres; each chart has its own fixed Y ceiling — `INTRADAY_SOLAR_MAX_W` (default **800** W, the PowerStream cap) and `INTRADAY_WATER_MAX_L` (default **150** L per 4h bucket), peaks clip. **No backfill**: strips accumulate day by day from deployment; today's fills live. Also fixes the water MQTT docstring (the meter reports every ≤30 min, not "a few frames per day"). Goldens (data + render) and preview regenerated.

- **`EDF` intraday strip: fixed Y scale (`INTRADAY_MAX_W`)** — the strips' heights no longer normalise to the 9-day window's max (which re-dilated the whole scale whenever a peak day slid out of the window) but to a **fixed absolute ceiling**, the new `INTRADAY_MAX_W` env var (default **3000** W of mean `PAPP` at full height). Day profiles now compare across renders, not just within one; a 4h mean above the ceiling clips to the strip's full height. Render golden and preview regenerated.
- **`EDF` intraday strip: sparkline-style bars** — the intraday profile under each bar drops its 28 thin 1px columns (hard to read on e-paper) for **6 four-hour bars** drawn with the bottom-table sparkline geometry (3px bars, 2px gaps — exactly the bar's 28px width). Heights still normalise to the 9-day window's global max, now computed on the **bucketed 4h means** so the window's tallest bar reaches the strip's full height; a bucket with no sample still leaves a gap (today's strip keeps filling live, bar by bar). Render golden and preview regenerated; no new environment variable.
- **`EDF` chart: intraday power profile under each bar** — each daily bar gains a **bar-wide (28px) mini bar-graph** squeezed between the bars' baseline and the day labels, showing *how* that day's power was drawn: the mean apparent power (`PAPP`) of each 30-min slot (the ZLinky's `tic_samples`), resampled to one 1px column per pixel. Heights normalise to the **9-day window's global max** so day profiles compare to each other — the night talon reads as a thin baseline, the cumulus/oven as spikes. Today's strip **fills live** through the day; a slot with no sample leaves a gap, and there is **no backfill** — days recorded before the ZLinky was connected show no strip, so the strips accumulate day by day from deployment. The bars give up ~18px of max height; the strip space is always reserved so the layout never jumps. Also fixes the preview script's EDF demo-week injection, dead since the calendar-anchored window (the dev DB's empty history rendered as 9 N/A columns). Goldens (data + render) and preview regenerated; no new environment variable.
- **Fix: `EDF` chart window anchored on the calendar** — the chart used to show the last 8 *rows present in the database*, so a missing day (server down, data purged) silently shifted the window one day back (the chart ended on Thursday with no trace of the absent Friday). `build_core` now walks the **8 calendar days before today** and fills holes with an empty row, which renders as the existing **N/A column marker** — a gap now reads as a gap. Goldens byte-identical (the test fixture has no hole); no new environment variable.

Driven by the ZLinky's live `PTEC` field (with the `HC_WINDOWS` clock as fallback) and recomputed at **every `/display` pull**, so it reads the exact moment the screen refreshed — then holds until the next refresh. No new environment variable; goldens and preview regenerated.
- **Whole-percent trends everywhere** — every trend on the dashboard (`EDF` stats, `Talon`, power sensors, `Solaire`, `Eau`, `Réseau`) now rounds to a **whole percent** at computation (no more `▼6.5%`); the alert figures inherit the integer values. A sub-0.5% drift rounds to 0 and renders as the em dash (no trend). Goldens regenerated.

- **Fix: ZLinky payload parsing** — Zigbee2MQTT translates the TIC labels to snake_case property names (`HCHC` → `current_tier1_summ_delivered`, `HCHP` → `current_tier2_summ_delivered`, `PAPP` → `apparent_power`, `PTEC` → `tariff_period`), so every frame failed the parser and `last_linky_message` stayed empty. The parser now reads the translated names (raw uppercase labels kept as fallback) and takes the live period from `tariff_period` — `current_tarif` is the OPTARIF contract constant, not the current period. Pinned on a payload captured from the live device.

- **⚠️ Linky data now read locally from a Lixee ZLinky_TIC over MQTT (breaking env change)** — the `EDF` consumption data no longer comes from the conso.boris.sh REST API but from a **ZLinky_TIC** Zigbee dongle plugged into the meter's TIC connector and published by Zigbee2MQTT (`LINKY_MQTT_TOPIC`, default `zigbee2mqtt/linky`, over the shared `MQTT_*` broker). Daily HC/HP kWh are now **exact** — computed as the deltas of the meter's cumulative `HCHC`/`HCHP` indexes since midnight (guarded against index resets and implausible jumps), instead of summing a 30-min load curve. A new **`tic_samples`** table keeps a fine-grained history (one row per 30-min slot: index snapshot + mean apparent power `PAPP`, unlimited retention); the **`Talon`** keeps its exact semantics (P20 of the night 23h–05h samples) but is now recomputed from those persisted slots, so a server restart no longer loses the night. The **off-peak split everywhere now follows the meter itself**: the live `PTEC` field ("HC.."/"HP..") decides whether a power sensor's increment counts as off-peak, with the `HC_WINDOWS` clock as fallback when the teleinfo goes quiet (>15 min) — so the `HC` column tracks what the meter actually billed, even if the contract windows change. There is **no backfill**: a day with the server down stays blank. Render layout and goldens are byte-identical. **On CasaOS: remove `LINKY_TOKEN` and `LINKY_PRM`; `MQTT_HOST` must be set (it now also feeds the core); add `LINKY_MQTT_TOPIC` only if your topic differs from `zigbee2mqtt/linky`.**

- **No more energy thresholds + em dash for uninitialised values** — every "too small = N/A" floor is removed: the `EDF` chart's **1 kWh** N/A floor, the `EDF` stats' 1 kWh valid-day filter and the `Solaire` (Solar) averages' **0.1 kWh** floor. Any positive value now draws its real bar and counts toward the averages; **N/A strictly means a null day** (no data at all — a meter gap, an inverter that never reported; a null solar day now shows `N/A` instead of a silent zero-height bar). Separately, a figure that *exists but isn't initialised yet* renders as an **em dash with its unit kept**: the `EDF` banner shows `—kWh/j` / `HC —%` / `—€/j` before its first valid day, and in the bottom table the `HC` cell shows `—` until post-deploy days accumulate, a yesterday with no report shows `— kWh` (distinct from a real `0 Wh` day) and a missing average / `Talon` shows `—` (was `N/A`). Rendered golden regenerated (data golden byte-identical); no new environment variable.
- **`Alertes`: per-plug off-peak drift rule + `Cumulus` section renamed `Prises`** — the status board's `Cumulus` section becomes **`Prises`** (Plugs): it now appears as soon as **any** power sensor is configured and hosts every power-sensor rule (the existing cumulus rise/drop included). New red rule **`prise_hc_drop`**: a plug whose **last-9-days off-peak share** (`hc_pct`) fell by **≥ 10 points** vs the prior 28 days fires *Heures creuses {nom} en baisse {n}pts* (off-peak share of {name} dropping), costed at the **HP premium** on the shifted kWh (`avg_kwh × points × (PRICE_HP − PRICE_HC)` per day); if several plugs drift, only the **worst offender** shows (vertical space). It needs both periods' HC split, so on an existing deployment it stays **quiet for ~5 weeks** while post-deploy history accumulates. Threshold: `PRISE_HC_DROP_PTS` in `electricity/alerts.py`. Goldens regenerated (the seed now drifts the washer out of off-peak to exercise the rule); no new environment variable.
- **`EDF` bottom table: off-peak `HC` % column** — each power-sensor row gains an **`HC` column**: the share (%) of its **last 9 days'** consumption that fell inside the Linky off-peak windows (`HC_WINDOWS`, the same variable the EDF chart already uses) — handy to check a `Cumulus` really heats off-peak. The MQTT integrator now splits each power increment into a new `daily_power.hc_wh` column alongside the daily total (idempotent migration; **no new environment variable**). Days recorded **before** this feature have no HC split and are excluded from the ratio, so the `HC` cell stays **empty until new days accumulate** after deployment (the Talon row never shows one — the talon is a power level, not an energy bucket).
- **`EDF` bottom table: section-title header + tighter layout** — the table now opens with its own **column-title row above the separator line** (`Hier` — yesterday, `Moy.` — average, `HC`), drawn in the **same band as the `Solaire` (Solar) title** opposite so both sections share the same separator line; the EDF chart's baseline and day labels now **align with the `Eau` (Water) chart's** to its right. The redundant `hier` word disappears from the row units (`3.2 kWh hier` → `3.2 kWh`, `303 W hier` → `303 W` — the header carries that meaning now), names truncate at **13 characters** (was 33) with an ellipsis, and the columns are spread out for air — except the average and its trend, which read as one glued block. Goldens (data + render) regenerated; the preview also gains extra representative demo rows (`Machine à laver`, `Serveur`, `Salon`, `Imprimante 3D`) to exercise the fuller table.

The **NTP** sync result is now checked and logged; with no valid clock the sleep lasts one **full interval** (instead of a hard-coded 1 h) and a later wake with NTP up realigns itself onto the even hours. New firmware constant `MAX_QUICK_RETRIES` — **reflash the ESP32** to enable.

## 2026-06-01

- **Solar "today" bar fix (real root cause)** — today's solar bar was stuck at `0` even though production was accumulating correctly in the database. The read-back query used an **exclusive** upper bound set to *today*, so `WHERE date < today` dropped today's own row and fell back to 0. The bound is now *tomorrow*, matching the water slice. (The earlier keep-alive/echo and threshold changes were genuine improvements but not the cause of this symptom — the `today` bar had never worked since it was introduced.)

- **Solar "today" bar fix** — today's solar bar showed **N/A** despite live production. The MQTT listener subscribes to the same topic it publishes its 60s keep-alive request to, so the broker echoed that request back; sharing the heartbeat's `cmd_func/cmd_id` but carrying an empty payload, it decoded to a phantom **0 W** sample that claimed the whole interval since the last real reading at zero watts, collapsing the day's integral to ~0. Payload-less control echoes are now ignored (a genuine night-time 0 W heartbeat still carries a full payload, so it is unaffected), and the listener no longer subscribes to the `…/property/get` topic it publishes to, removing the echo at its source.
- **Solar chart shows every yield** — the solar chart no longer hides small days behind an `N/A` floor: any value, however small (down to 0), now draws a bar. The `N/A` threshold still applies to the EDF consumption chart only — useful for small PV setups whose daily yield can sit below the old 0.05 kWh floor.
- **Water meter consumption** — optional `Eau` (Water) chart in the top-center space, between the Solar chart and the Crypto panel: the last **9 days** of water use as daily-litres bars, with a title showing the average **L/day** (and trend), the month-to-date **m³** and its **€** cost. An ESPHome wM-Bus reader publishes the meter's cumulative index (m³) over MQTT; the dashboard derives daily litres by **index difference** (not power integration, unlike Cumulus) — no backfill, history starts at first connection. A day with no reading shows **N/A**, including today until its first frame arrives. Enabled by setting `WATER_MQTT_HOST`.
